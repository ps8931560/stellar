Recognition

Stellar EduConsultancy Honored at Kyrgyz Republic Med Expo 2025

We are proud to share that Mr. Rafique Nasroddin Shaikh, representing Stellar Science Hub & Stellar EduConsultancy, was felicitated as a Guest of Honor at the Kyrgyz Republic Med Expo 2025, organized by MT Patil Education.

This recognition reflects our continued commitment to guiding Indian students towards quality medical education abroad and strengthening international academic collaboration. The Med Expo 2025 brought together reputed universities, education leaders, and industry experts to discuss opportunities in global medical education, with a special focus on Kyrgyzstan and other emerging destinations for MBBS.

Our participation in such prestigious platforms allows us to stay connected with global trends, build strong institutional relationships, and provide our students with the most updated and authentic information. Being invited as a Guest of Honor is not just a personal milestone for Mr. Rafique Nasroddin Shaikh, but also a testament to the trust our students and partners have placed in Stellar EduConsultancy.

We remain dedicated to our mission of making quality medical education accessible, transparent and hassle-free for every aspiring doctor.

“This honor motivates us to work even harder towards empowering students and building brighter futures.”

— Mr. Rafique Nasroddin Shaikh
