02—Why NMC Matters

Your licence to practise in India depends on it.

The National Medical Commission (NMC) regulates which foreign medical degrees are recognised in India. Graduating from an unapproved university means you cannot sit the the applicable screening test (currently FMGE) exam — and you cannot practise medicine in India.

We review the available WDOMS entry and programme details against the FMGL requirements. Students should independently verify current eligibility before admission.

Programme Compliance is Mandatory

Indian registration depends on satisfying the applicable FMGL rules, completing the required internship and licensing pathway, and verifying the institution’s current information.

WDOMS Listing Must be Active

Listings and programme details can change. We review available information and ask students to verify the current WDOMS entry and FMGL requirements before admission.

FMGE Eligibility Requires It

NBEMS continues to conduct the FMGE licensing examination. Students should check the latest NMC and NBEMS notices before relying on an examination pathway.
