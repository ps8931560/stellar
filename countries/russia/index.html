07 — Student Life

Life in Russia

climate

Winters vary: −10°C in southern cities, −25°C in Siberian cities. Essential to budget ₹15,000–₹20,000 for winter gear in Year 1. Most students adapt within one semester.

food

Indian mess and restaurants available in all major university cities. Indian groceries widely available.

safety

Generally safe. University campuses have security. Travel in groups recommended in initial months.

transport

Excellent metro systems in major cities. Buses and trams in smaller cities.

currency

Russian Ruble (RUB). Monthly living expense ~₹15,000–₹20,000.

10 — FAQs

FAQ — MBBS in Russia

Which Russian university has the best FMGE pass rate?
Kazan State Medical University (est. 1814) leads with 45–65% FMGE pass rate. Omsk State (45–55%) and Volgograd (40–50%) are also strong. Budget universities like Kemerovo (20–35%) score lower.
Is MBBS from Russia valid in India?
Indian registration depends on the programme meeting the applicable FMGL requirements and on completing the screening or licensing pathway in force when you graduate.
How cold is Russia? Can I survive?
It varies by city. Volgograd and Omsk are moderate (−10°C to −15°C). Siberian cities like Kemerovo can hit −25°C. With proper winter gear (budget ₹15,000–₹20,000), most students adapt in their first semester.
What is the FMGE pass rate from Russia?
It depends heavily on the university. Top universities: Kazan (45–65%), Omsk (45–55%), Volgograd (40–50%). Mid-tier: Tyumen (30–45%), North Ossetia (25–40%). Budget: Kemerovo (20–35%), Chechen (15–30%). University selection is critical.
Do I need to learn Russian?
Teaching is in English. Basic Russian is helpful for clinical interactions in later years — universities provide language courses in Year 1 and 2.
