The Process

Admissions & Relocation Roadmap

01

Book a session for free

A focused free session with our expert consultant. In just 60 minutes, walk away with clarity on your NEET score fit, budget plan, country choice, and a personalised university shortlist — zero fluff, zero upsell. Most students save over ₹1 lakh in costly mistakes from this one conversation.

02

Eligibility Review

We check NEET, PCB, age, and whether an Eligibility Certificate is required for the student’s specific admission history. Processing: 10–15 working days.

03

University Shortlisting

Receive 2–3 personalised university recommendations with full reasoning — fees, FMGE pass rates, location, and total cost. No hidden charges.

04

Application & Offer Letter

Document submission with our assistance. Receive official Invitation or Offer Letter within 2–4 weeks while NMC application runs in parallel.

05

Visa Application

Country-specific documentation and embassy submission. Processing varies: 2–6 weeks. Medical examination required for Russia, Kazakhstan, and Uzbekistan.

06

Pre-Departure Briefing

Complete packing list, currency exchange guidance, emergency contacts, and your FMGE preparation strategy — a study plan built before Day 1.

07

Arrival & Settling In

Airport pickup arranged at most partner universities. Hostel check-in, university registration, orientation, and introduction to the Indian student community.

08

Ongoing Support

Semester check-ins throughout your MBBS, FMGE orientation every year, PG planning as you near graduation, and emergency support — always reachable.
